\section{Simulation Study}
\label{simulation}

As the ensemble data described in \cref{case} cannot be published we instead provide a simulated example, with an identical framework.

The observational data, data split, training methodology, etc. are kept identical with the case study, for details refer to \cref{case-data}. 

The Only change is to replace the original, proprietary ensembles with simulated data.

\subsection{Simulated ensembles}

To replace the ensembles we simulated en Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) mean-reverting process, augmented with a process driven heteroscedasticity.

\begin{align}
    dX &= \kappa\big(\mu(t) - X\big)\,dt + \sigma(t)\, dB_t. \\
    d\sigma &= -\lambda \sigma dt + (1-\lambda)d\mu dt
\end{align}

To drive the process The actual observed data was used for the mean process. To simplify and standardize the code, observations were scaled to the interval [0,1]:

\begin{align}
    \mu(t) &= \frac{y_t}{c_{zone}} \\
    C_{zone} = \max y_i
\end{align}

Simulation was performed using a Euler-Maruyama scheme, with of $dt = 1$.

\begin{align}
    X_t &= X_{t-1} + \kappa(\mu_{t} - X_{t-1}) + \sigma_t\epsilon_t \\
    \sigma_t &= \sigma_{t-1} (1 - \lambda) + (1-\lambda)d\mu
\end{align}

where $\epsilon_t \sim N(0,1)$ and IID.

For this simulation study the parameters $\kappa = 0.1$ and $\lambda = 0.95$ was chosen.

After simulation the simulated ensembles were clipped to the range [0,1] and the scaled back to original scale, to mimic the original ensembles.

\begin{equation}
    \tilde X_t = C_{zone} \min(0, \max(1, X_t)
\end{equation}
